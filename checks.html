All 74 checks

What Odoo Doctor looks at, and why. 21 of them are in the free Odoo Doctor Lite; the rest are in the full Odoo Doctor. Severity is the default: it can be changed per check in the full version.

Security · System & performance · Accounting · Data quality · Sales & purchase

Security 11

Database manager exposed

Critical Lite and Odoo Doctor

The database manager (/web/database) can download, duplicate or delete entire databases. Whoever knows the master password controls all your data.

Administrators without two-factor login

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

A stolen or guessed password is enough to take over an administrator account unless two-factor authentication is turned on.

Archived employees who can still log in

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Archiving an employee does not switch off their login. Until the user is archived too, a person who left can still sign in and see company data, and the account still uses a paid user seat.

Too many administrators

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Administrators can read every record, install modules and change security settings. Each extra admin account is an extra way in.

Default "admin" login still in use

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

Attackers try the login "admin" first. Renaming it removes half of what they need to guess.

Demo data installed

Medium Odoo Doctor

Demo data adds fake customers, products, users and journal entries that mix with your real data and distort reports. It should never be in a production database.

Dormant internal users

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

Active accounts nobody uses (often ex-employees) keep access to your data and cost you a paid user seat.

Odoo connects to PostgreSQL as a superuser

Medium Odoo Doctor

Odoo's deployment guide says the PostgreSQL user must not be a superuser. If Odoo is ever attacked through its database connection, a superuser can read and write files on the database server.

Several databases and no database filter

Medium Odoo Doctor

Odoo's deployment guide says a production server with several databases must set a database filter (dbfilter). Without it, any address can open any of the databases.

Anyone can sign up

Low Odoo Doctor

With free sign-up, anyone on the internet can create a portal account. Fine for e-commerce, risky for everyone else.

Old API keys without expiry

Low Odoo Doctor

API keys work like passwords that never expire. Old keys are often forgotten in scripts, laptops and integrations.

System & performance 32

Database connections nearly full

High Odoo Doctor

When PostgreSQL runs out of connections, users get errors and Odoo stops responding until connections are freed.

Installed modules missing from the server

High Odoo Doctor

The database says these modules are installed but their code is not on the server. This is the classic "module not found" after moving to a new server, and an upgrade cannot succeed until the code is back or the module is uninstalled.

Low disk space for uploaded files

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

When the disk fills up, uploads, reports and backups fail, and PostgreSQL can stop. It is one of the most common causes of a sudden outage.

Modules stuck mid-install or mid-upgrade

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

A module left in "to install", "to upgrade" or "to remove" will be processed at the next restart, sometimes with surprising results.

Odoo version out of standard support

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Odoo gives each version three years of standard support, with bug fixes and security updates. After that, fixes depend on an extended-support contract and the version becomes harder to upgrade.

Outgoing email queue is stuck

High Odoo Doctor

Emails have been waiting in the queue too long. Usually the mail server or the email scheduled action is down.

Records whose file is missing from the filestore

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Attachments, images and generated PDFs that Odoo still lists but can no longer open. Usually the database was restored without its filestore, or from a different moment than the filestore.

Scheduled actions are not running

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Scheduled actions send emails, post recurring entries, follow up on invoices and clean up the database. When they stop, things silently break.

Users waiting on database locks

High Odoo Doctor

Sessions waiting for a lock held by another session look like a frozen screen to the user. Find the session that holds the lock and why it is slow.

Database is marked as neutralized

Medium Odoo Doctor

Neutralizing a database copy switches off outgoing email, scheduled actions and payment providers so a test copy cannot contact real customers. If a neutralized copy ends up in production, nothing is sent and nothing runs.

Developer options switched on

Medium Odoo Doctor

The --dev option reloads code on every change, shows full error details to users and turns off caching. It is meant for developers, and makes a live server slower and less safe.

Emails that failed to send

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

Invoices, quotations and notifications that customers never received.

Files stored inside the database

Medium Odoo Doctor

Files stored in the database make it larger, slower, and backups take much longer.

Invalid database indexes

Medium Odoo Doctor

An index left invalid by an interrupted build is never used for searches but is still updated on every write, so it only costs time and disk space.

Odoo runs without workers

Medium Odoo Doctor

Without workers, Odoo handles one request at a time: users wait for each other, long reports block everyone and scheduled actions compete with users. Fine for testing, not for production.

Odoo's own cleanup job is turned off

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

The scheduled action "Base: Auto-vacuum internal data" removes old sessions, login logs, bus messages, expired API keys and orphaned files. Without it these tables and the filestore keep growing.

Outdated PostgreSQL version

Medium Odoo Doctor

PostgreSQL versions past their end of life no longer get security fixes.

Queries running for a very long time

Medium Odoo Doctor

Queries that run for minutes use a database connection and a CPU the whole time, and usually mean a missing index or a report that is too large.

Reverse proxy not declared (proxy mode off)

Medium Odoo Doctor

Odoo does not serve https itself, so an https address means a reverse proxy sits in front of it. Without proxy mode Odoo ignores the headers the proxy sends and sees every visitor as the proxy.

Scheduled actions failing

Medium Odoo Doctor

These scheduled actions raise errors when they run. Odoo will eventually deactivate them automatically.

Sessions stuck inside an open transaction

Medium Odoo Doctor

A session that opened a transaction and then stopped keeps its locks and stops PostgreSQL from cleaning up old rows. Usually an import, an integration waiting on an outside service, or a script that was never closed.

Workers have no memory limit

Medium Odoo Doctor

With no memory limit, one runaway report or import can use all the RAM of the server and take everything else down with it.

Wrong or insecure base URL

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

The base URL is used in every link Odoo sends: emails, portal invitations, password resets and payment links.

Base URL can change by itself

Low Odoo Doctor

Odoo rewrites the base URL whenever an administrator logs in, using the address typed in the browser. Log in once through an IP address or a test domain and every link in emails, portal invitations and payment requests changes with it.

Tables that need cleaning (VACUUM)

Low Odoo Doctor

Deleted and updated rows stay on disk until PostgreSQL cleans them. Large amounts slow down every search on those tables.

Workers do not fit the server

Low Odoo Doctor

Too many workers for the CPUs make requests slower, and too many for the memory let the system kill Odoo (or PostgreSQL) when the server runs out of RAM.

Custom fields and models

Info Odoo Doctor

Fields and models created in the interface (their names start with x_), for example with Studio. They are the lower-risk part of an upgrade, but they still need testing afterwards.

Database size

Info Odoo Doctor

The overall size of the database and its largest tables.

File storage size

Info Odoo Doctor

How much space attachments take, and which documents use the most.

Modules not made by Odoo

Info Odoo Doctor

Every module that is not part of Odoo must exist for the version you upgrade to. This is the list to give your developer or Odoo partner before an upgrade.

Python code stored in the database

Info Odoo Doctor

Server actions with Python code live in the database, not in a module. They are outside Odoo's standard upgrade and are not under version control, so someone has to test them after an upgrade.

Unused database indexes

Info Odoo Doctor

Indexes that were never used since the statistics were last reset still slow down every write and take disk space. Statistics reset when PostgreSQL restarts, so check again after the server has run for a while.

Accounting 15

Unbalanced journal entries

Critical Lite and Odoo Doctor

Posted entries where debit does not equal credit. Your balance sheet and every report built on it are wrong.

Possible duplicate vendor bills

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Posted bills from the same vendor with the same reference. These are very often paid twice.

Closed periods are not locked

Medium Odoo Doctor

Without a lock date, anyone with accounting rights can change entries in years you already reported to the tax office.

Foreign currencies with outdated rates

Medium Odoo Doctor

Invoices in a foreign currency are converted with the latest rate Odoo has. Without a recent rate, revenue, costs and exchange differences are misstated.

Gaps in invoice numbering

Medium Odoo Doctor

Many countries require customer invoices to be numbered without gaps, and auditors ask about every missing number. Do not renumber posted invoices: document why each number is missing (for example a cancelled draft) and ignore this finding with that reason if your country does not require gapless numbers.

Old draft journal entries

Medium Odoo Doctor

Draft entries are left out of every report. Old drafts are usually forgotten invoices or bills.

Suspense and transfer accounts are not at zero

Medium Odoo Doctor

Money parked in a suspense or internal transfer account has not been matched to anything yet. Odoo's year-end checklist says to close all suspense accounts.

The same bank account on different partners

Medium Odoo Doctor

When two vendors share one bank account, one of them is a duplicate, a mistake, or a fraud: money meant for one supplier ends up with another.

Unreconciled bank transactions

Medium Odoo Doctor

Bank transactions that were never matched to an invoice or payment. Your receivables, payables and cash position are not reliable.

Vendor bank details changed recently

Medium Odoo Doctor

A common fraud: someone pretends to be a supplier and asks to change the bank account. Confirm every change by phone, using a number you already had, before the next payment.

Vendor bills that look like duplicates

Medium Odoo Doctor

The same vendor and amount a few days apart, or the same reference written differently ("INV-001" and "inv001"). Exact repeats are caught by a separate check.

Long-overdue customer invoices

Low Odoo Doctor

Money customers owe you that is at high risk of never being paid.

Payments never matched with the bank

Low Odoo Doctor

Payments that were recorded in Odoo but never appeared on a bank statement. Cheques that were never cashed, or payments that were never really made.

Sellable products without sales tax

Low Odoo Doctor

Products that can be sold but have no customer tax. Unless they are really tax exempt, you are under-charging tax.

Vendor bills overdue for payment

Low Odoo Doctor

Bills you owe and have not paid. Late payments cost fees and damage the relationship with the supplier.

Data quality 10

Negative stock

High Lite and Odoo Doctor

Negative quantities on hand mean receipts were not recorded or deliveries were validated twice. Inventory valuation is wrong.

Serial numbers in more than one place

High Odoo Doctor

One serial number is one physical unit in one place. A serial number with a quantity above 1, or in several locations at once, means stock was received or counted wrongly.

Duplicate partners (same tax ID)

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

The same company entered twice splits its invoices, payments and credit limit across two records.

Tracked products in stock without a lot or serial number

Medium Odoo Doctor

Products set to lot or serial tracking that have stock without a number. This usually starts when tracking is switched on after stock already exists.

Companies without a country

Low Odoo Doctor

The country drives fiscal positions, taxes and e-invoicing. A missing country often means the wrong tax.

Company details left as placeholders

Low Lite and Odoo Doctor

The company name and address are printed on every invoice, quotation and email. A name like "My Company" or an empty address looks unprofessional.

Duplicate contacts (same email)

Low Odoo Doctor

Separate companies sharing one email address are usually duplicates created by imports, the website or email gateways.

Duplicate contacts (same phone)

Low Odoo Doctor

Separate companies sharing one phone number are usually the same customer entered twice, with different spacing or country codes.

Invalid email addresses

Low Lite and Odoo Doctor

Invoices and quotations sent to these contacts will bounce.

Products sold without a cost

Low Odoo Doctor

Goods with a sales price but a cost of zero show 100% margin in every report and are valued at zero in stock.

Sales & purchase 6

Online payments waiting for confirmation

Medium Odoo Doctor

Payments the provider never confirmed, and payments that were authorized but never captured. Often the provider cannot reach Odoo (a webhook problem), so paid orders stay unpaid. Bank transfers are left out: they wait for you.

Payment providers in test mode

Medium Odoo Doctor

A provider in test mode takes no real money. If it is shown to customers, they can "pay" without paying anything.

Purchases waiting for a vendor bill

Medium Odoo Doctor

Goods or services received without a recorded bill make your costs and payables look lower than they are, and the bill may surprise you later.

Sales orders waiting to be invoiced

Medium Lite and Odoo Doctor

Confirmed or delivered orders that were never invoiced are revenue you are not collecting.

Transfers long past their scheduled date

Medium Odoo Doctor

Deliveries and receipts that are still open long after they were due. Customers are waiting, or the stock was never booked and reservations are blocking other orders.

Old quotations never followed up

Low Odoo Doctor

Open quotations nobody followed up on are lost deals and clutter the pipeline.
